Part 1 · My Wellness Activity

Walking

I chose walking because it works your whole body and needs no gym, no equipment and no special skill — just a pair of shoes and somewhere to go. You can do it at school, at home, at the mall or in the park.

“Walking is the most commonly reported physical activity by U.S. adults.” — Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), MMWR report

Part 2 · Research

Physical Health Benefits

Stronger Heart

Walking just 21 minutes a day “can reduce your risk of heart attack by 30%.”

Mayo Clinic Health System ↗

Calories & Endurance

Brisk walking can “improve your heart health and increase your endurance while burning calories.”

Mayo Clinic ↗

Blood Pressure & Sugar

Regular walking helps control blood pressure, helps people manage diabetes and lowers the risk of type 2 diabetes.

Mayo Clinic Health System ↗

Muscles, Core & Balance

Walking improves balance and coordination, strengthens your core and keeps your muscles strong and mobile.

Mayo Clinic Health System ↗

Longer, Healthier Life

Regular walking is linked to a lower risk of early death, healthier arteries and less weight gain. Talking about walking for people with heart disease, cardiologist Dr. Hicham Skali said: “It’s better than any pill out there.”

Mass General Brigham ↗

Part 4 · Creative Ideas

Walking in My Daily Routine

“One way I can walk during my day is at home I can go on a walk for a bit.”

When I get home from school, I can put my shoes on, grab some water and take a short walk around my neighborhood. Even 10 to 20 minutes helps me get moving after sitting in class all day and clears my head before homework.
