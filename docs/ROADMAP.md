# Dust & Badge — Build Roadmap

Built in small, playable stages. Each stage is pushed to the repo when it's done.

## Stage 1 — Town & Badlands (done)
- Title screen with 3 save slots, intro cutscene (bank robbery, "Silver" Jack)
- Redemption Gulch: Sheriff's Office (sleep/save), Bank, General Store (buy/sell,
  leather gloves), Saloon & Hotel (room, meal, hints), Stable (empty for now), townsfolk
- The Badlands desert road with 4 abandoned houses to enter and loot
  (some have a rattlesnake or squatter inside)
- Fist fighting: punch, block, perfect block (dazes the enemy), enemy wind-ups
- Enemies: rattlesnakes, outlaws, coyotes (night only, afraid of campfires)
- Day/night clock (30 real minutes per day), lantern and campfire lighting
- Hunger (drains faster in the desert heat) and tiredness meters
- Campfire anywhere: sleep, save, respawn point; death respawns at last rest spot
- Chiptune music for town, desert day, night, fights; sound effects
- Keyboard + mouse and touch controls
- Road ends at a rockslide (Canyon Pass) for now

## Stage 2 — Interiors, townsfolk & horses (done)
- Walk-in interiors for every town building: Sheriff's Office (desk, wanted
  posters, jail cell cot, Deputy Ned), General Store (shelves, counter, Martha),
  Saloon & Hotel (bar, bottles, piano, stairs, Gus, Miss Lila, Slick Doyle),
  Bank (teller cage, open vault, Eustace), Stable (stalls, Pete)
- Unique townsfolk bodies: bowler hats, top hats, dresses, bald bartender with
  apron, bearded prospector, long-coated doctor and gambler, deputy, kid
- 6 abandoned building types along the road (8 buildings): shack, farmhouse,
  adobe ruin, barn, way station, chapel, each with its own interior style and
  searchable furniture (wardrobes, desks, bookshelves, clay pots, sacks, hay,
  offering box...)
- Horses from Pete's stable: mule, bay horse, black stallion; name your horse,
  ride (H), gallop (Shift), whistle it over, horse hunger bar, horse feed,
  trade-ins. No jumping or fighting in the saddle.

## Stage 2.5 — A living town (done)
- Every building redrawn to scale with real detail (siding, cornices, shutters,
  curtains, awnings, balconies, brick, steeples, hanging signs, chimneys)
- New town buildings: Doctor's Office, Post & Telegraph, Blacksmith, Church
  (all walk-in), and the stable rebuilt as a livery barn
- Street life: more townsfolk with daily schedules and greetings, Rusty the
  dog, chickens, horses at hitching posts, chimney smoke, the blacksmith's
  hammer, lamp posts, a water tower, windmill, well, wagon, corral

## Stage 2.6 — New look (done)
- Characters from the Mucho Pixels character pack, about 60px tall, repainted
  with western outfits (stetsons, bowlers, top hats, bonnets, vests, badges,
  boots, skirts, aprons, beards, outlaw bandanas)
- Make-your-sheriff screen on a new game (body, skin, hair, hat, facial hair),
  also in the pause menu
- Every building redrawn as a chunky storefront: bold outlines, false fronts,
  big signs, striped awnings, balconies; taller doors for the bigger people
- One pixel size for everything (2x2 screen pixels per art pixel): props,
  animals, horses, sky, mesas, interiors, effects

## Stage 3 — The Canyon & Silverbend (see VISION.md, "Area 2")
- 3.1 (done): the road stays blocked by the rockslide; a slot in the cliff
  beside it leads into Canyon Pass, its own long side-scrolling map (press
  USE at the slot; your horse comes along). Deep red canyon walls, dry
  riverbed, rock spires and boulders, rockfalls, more frequent and tougher
  enemies, canyon music ("Lasso Lady"), "Prairie - coming soon" sign at the
  far end. Saves remember which map you're on.
- 3.1 (done): Silverbend, the silver-mining town half way along: Assay
  Office, Supply Co., The Silver Spur saloon with rooms, Doc Ames, the
  Livery, and the Silver Mine (shut for now). Its own townsfolk, miners,
  a dog called Nugget, ore cart and tailings.
- 3.1b (done): very tight canyon (near walls, overhang, sliver of sky, shadowed
  floor, foreground rock). Silverbend rebuilt as a cliff-walled pocket that
  climbs its back wall (cliff houses, walkways, ladders, headframe, flume),
  with a ledge you climb to by ladder (Bunkhouse, Clinic) over the mine.
  Custom mining interiors: Assay Office, The Silver Spur, Supply Co.,
  Bunkhouse (free sleep/save), Clinic, Livery. Miners swing picks and push
  ore carts. Canyon details: campsites, broken wagons, claim stakes, gear,
  rope bridges overhead, tracks and cracks, birds, lizards, swaying brush,
  trickling dust.
- 3.2: scorpions, mountain lions, canyon outlaws
- 3.3: canyon loot spots: cliff dwelling, miner's cabin, wagon camp, mine shed
- 3.5: the walk-in mine, and the "close call" cutscene with Jack
